Perennial ryegrass (*Lolium perenne*) is a major turfgrass and forage species valued for its fast establishment and high forage quality, yet traditional transformation protocols remain inefficient and strongly genotype dependent. To address chronic regeneration bottlenecks in callus culture, we tested constructs driving maize developmental regulators (*ZmWUS2* and *ZmBBM*) under various promoters—with and without Cre recombinase excision—to induce somatic embryogenesis and enable genotype-independent transformation.
